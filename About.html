About MediFriend

MediFriend is an educational tool that helps you explore common conditions, understand their symptoms, and learn about prevention and treatment options. It is not a diagnostic tool and should never replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Key Conditions Covered

Below are a few examples of conditions included in this project, along with their most common symptoms.

Cholera

  • Watery diarrhea
  • Vomiting
  • Dehydration
  • Muscle cramps

Genital Herpes

  • Painful blisters
  • Itching
  • Burning sensation

Food Poisoning

  • Vomiting
  • Stomach pain
  • Tiredness

Flu

  • Fever
  • Cough
  • Sore throat
  • Tiredness

To learn more about any of these conditions, go to the Diagnosis page and click a card to watch a short educational video.
